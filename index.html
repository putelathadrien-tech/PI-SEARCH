How does it work?

Every letter is turned into numbers, then we look for that string of numbers in pi. Positions are counted from the first digit after the decimal point (3.1415… is position 1).

  • Alphabet: A=01, B=02, … Z=26. ADA becomes 010401.
  • Phone keypad: one digit per letter, like old phone keyboards. HELLO becomes 43556. Shorter codes mean longer words can be found.

Sentences: we first look for the whole sentence (spaces ignored). Long sentences need far more digits than exist here, so each word is also found on its own. If a word is still too long, it is split into the longest pieces that do appear in pi.

Digits of pi computed with the Chudnovsky algorithm. All searching happens in your browser.
