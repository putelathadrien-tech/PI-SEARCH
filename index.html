♛ House Rules ♛

Every letter is turned into numbers, then we hunt for those numbers in the digits of pi. Positions are counted from the first digit after the point (3.1415… is position 1).

  • A=01 … Z=26: ADA becomes 010401, the same rule as pinames.org.
  • Phone keypad: one digit per letter (ABC=2 … WXYZ=9). HELLO becomes 43556. Shorter codes mean bigger words can win.
  • Sentences: we try the whole sentence first, then deal out every word as its own card. Words too long to find are split into the longest pieces that do appear in pi.

Just for fun: no money, no bets, only digits. Everything runs in your browser.
